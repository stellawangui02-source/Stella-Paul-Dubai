UAE Tourist Visa — Apply in Advance

~AED 350–500 + service fees · Standard processing about 3–5 working days

  • •Kenyan passport holders don't currently qualify for UAE visa-on-arrival, so this needs sorting before 25 September.
  • ✓Since you're flying Emirates, the simplest route is applying through Emirates directly once your ticket is issued — they process it via VFS Global.
  • •Kenyan passport, valid 6+ months from travel date (bio page copy).
  • •Recent passport-style photo, white background, for each traveler.
  • ✓Confirmed Emirates return ticket (EK718 / EK721).
  • ✓Asiana Grand Hotel booking confirmation.
  • •Marriage certificate — not mandatory, but worth including since you're applying as a couple, especially if your surnames differ.

Keep this page spare and quick to scan.
